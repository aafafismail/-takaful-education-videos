## Maths check
- RM100 ÷ 30 = RM3.333… ≈ **RM3.33** a day
- 8 × RM13 = **RM104** (covers RM100)
- RM13 − RM3 (home coffee, illustrative) = **RM10** saved per switch; 10 × RM10 = **RM100**
- RM13 − RM9.50 = **RM3.50**

All numbers on screen are calculated from `prices` in `src/latte/config.ts`.
