# Voiceover script: Couples arguing over household finances

This is a script for a real human recording. It is not a transcript and it is not synthesised.

Total: 54 words, about 22 s at a calm pace. Keep the full recording between 19 and 29 s.

How to record
- Quiet room, phone 20 cm from your mouth, one take per scene or one continuous take.
- Warm, conversational, unhurried. Small pause between scenes.
- Save as WAV or M4A in briefs/media/, set media.voiceover.path in the brief, then rebuild.
- For captions that match the audio exactly, also export an SRT from your editor and set media.voiceover.srt.

## Scene 1 · hook · 0.0s to 6.4s

Kadang bukan duit yang buat pasangan bergaduh. Tapi cara kita bercakap pasal duit.

## Scene 2 · message · 6.0s to 11.7s

Sebelum bincang siapa belanja apa, senaraikan komitmen bersama dulu. Tanpa salahkan sesiapa.

## Scene 3 · tip · 11.2s to 18.2s

Luangkan lima belas minit. Tulis bil rumah, barang dapur dan komitmen lain.

## Scene 4 · reinforce · 17.8s to 23.2s

Bila semua dah tertulis, anda berdua nampak gambaran yang sama.

## Scene 5 · closing · 22.7s to 27.5s

Cuba minggu ini. No blaming, just understanding.
